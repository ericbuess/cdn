# Categories

The initial taxonomy for `content/manifest.json`. Rename or replace any of these; `content/CATEGORIES.md`, the `taxonomy` array in the manifest and `tools/x-categorize.py`'s keyword table are the three places a category name appears.

- **safety-argument** — the case for taking AI risk seriously, and the counter-arguments I am still trying to answer.
- **hardening** — turning found weaknesses into tested, deployed protection for real software.
- **adversarial-safety** — red-teaming, independent challenge and what breaks when a system is pushed on purpose.
- **alignment-hypothesis** — developmental environments, persistent consequences and legitimate correction as a research programme.
- **fleet-and-tools** — the working system: agents, harnesses, voice, and the tooling that keeps work moving between them.
- **family-and-life** — being present, going screenless, and what I want the technology to make room for.
- **music** — songs, lyrics and audio experiments.
- **misc** — everything genuinely uncategorised; a row here is a prompt to pick a better home for it, not a permanent answer.
