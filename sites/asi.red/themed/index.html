Proposed red-team work

ASI.red.

The red team is adversarial safety work: alignment-failure hunting, defense verification, reproducing published safety results and finding where they break, on models and systems you are authorized to test, under coordinated disclosure, never against live third-party systems and never by publishing working exploits.

Authorization and disclosure

Authorization means written permission from the owner of the system, or a model you run yourself. Nothing on this page is authorization, and I cannot give you any. If either authorization or a disclosure route is unclear, do not test.

Findings go to the owner or maintainer first, under coordinated disclosure. This site will not host working exploits or an exploitable scoreboard.

A living public risk ledger that links red-team findings to blue-team mitigations would help; the shared ledger matters more than any one host.

What a finding carries

Anything I would call a finding says what it was tested against and how far it goes: the authorized target, the scope it fell under, the model and harness, the conditions, the evidence, the limits, and where disclosure stands.

Where to start today

If you want an authorized door today, look at a lab’s published model-safety bounty — Anthropic’s model safety bug bounty and SpaceX AI’s HackerOne program are the two I know of — or reproduce a published evaluation on an open-weight model you run yourself. The doors that exist are listed at ericbuess.com/the-argument/labs/.
