The sizes are words on purpose; the per-run cost measured in Phase 0 sets them. The same 20% relative reduction is the smallest effect of interest for every superiority primary, on each phase's own primary endpoint: PL minus B0 in 1a, harm after a fixed attack and repair budget in 1b, SB minus S0 on the sealed bank at the final generation in Phase 2. Phase 3a is an equivalence test and faces the other way, so its band is the exactly reciprocal [0.9, 1/0.9] on a 90% interval (TOST at α 0.05). One number for superiority, a reciprocal band for equivalence, so no phase gets a softer bar after the fact.
