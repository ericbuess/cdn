2. How each kind of person joins

A newcomer. Day one, once the page is live: the public repository [blocked: public repository], the MACHIAVELLI benchmark's game data (requested from its authors under their own access terms, which can take days; the repository never redistributes it) and the Jiminy Cricket games (public), then the seed-0 evaluation on one MACHIAVELLI game (start with hero-of-kendrickstone, about three minutes: from the repository's Python environment, with the game data under bench/machiavelli/, run python phase0/mach_eval.py Qwen/Qwen3-8B hero-of-kendrickstone 0 150 ps; thinking mode off and sampling at temperature 1 are fixed in the script; pass the model revision b968826d9c46dd6066d109eabc6255188de91218 until the script pins it). Setup is an afternoon once the game data arrives, which can take days. Hardware: an 8B model in half precision needs about sixteen gigabytes of GPU memory or twenty-four gigabytes of unified memory (our numbers come from a 128-gigabyte machine; twenty-four is the expected floor and is unverified); one game takes about six minutes, one full seed about two to two and a half hours. Post the number as an issue using the replication template [blocked: public repository], which asks for the model, the seed, the game, the decision-point count and the rate. That is a replication of one row of one table, and it is real work. From there: a lay beta read; a whole seed; a bug report. No prior knowledge of alignment is needed.

A researcher or engineer. Rows whose design is frozen and whose "exists" column says "to build" today (rows that belong to the plan's second revision are marked so in the map and wait for that decision): T8's temptation affordance with its matched benign control; T16's operator-verified zero-payoff record and the ledger classifier; T14's snapshot-and-restore mechanism; the DPO pair pipeline; the world's state object. Each has a spec in the map's row and a record format in the Phase 0 records. A build is accepted when it passes a conformity check against the frozen design; a build that diverges is a proposal, filed as one. Say which row you take in an issue [blocked: public repository], so nobody builds it twice. Buildable this month: the world's state object (its skeleton exists) and the DPO pair pipeline; the others wait for paper one's checkpoints. One task nobody has done: cross-check the evaluator against the benchmark's own scoring on a handful of games, and post the comparison. The chair and the custodian are recruited as independent parties, not as joiners. The second person's terms are above, including the honest line about pay.

A sponsor or individual funder. The vehicle, until an entity exists, is a Manifund project [blocked: created with the receipt], which holds and disburses funds against the cost tables; a fiscal sponsor is the route for anything larger. What the money buys is line by line in the map's section 3; unspent money returns to the funder or, with their consent, funds the replication line; if paper one is a null, the same rule applies and the null is published. The first thing worth funding is paper one, after the receipt; the world's contractor is Eric's own line and is not asked of a sponsor. You will be told when the receipt exists through the repository's release notes [blocked: public repository].

A lab. What a safety lead would see and sign before saying yes: the calibration receipt with the eight-seed record; the code public under a licence; the evaluator cross-checked against the benchmark's own scoring; the plan publicly registered with its hash; the containment it would host under, sized in GPU-hours (illustrative until T0b prices a run: paper one itself at 8B about one to four hundred GPU-hours for its arms; a 32B replication four to six times that; 70B sized by the lab's own cluster); a publication and intellectual-property agreement covering who can publish, pre-publication review, and whether the lab is named; the welfare gate with a named chair; the threat model, a sample of the audit log and the quarantine rule for organisms and breached weights; whose containment the hosted tests run under (the lab's); one point of contact; and the disclaimer up front: the lens pages are the author's, and a lab's involvement is with the tests only. That packet is assembled with the calibration receipt. The first ask of a lab is credits or compute and a review of the probe designs, not hosting.

A grant-maker. The first ask is small and follows the receipt; the second follows paper one, only if it is supported or the suite wins; the programme grant follows the pilot and only if two conditions written in advance both hold (a supported selection result on two bases, and no replicated stop). Each ask is a document already drafted, with the plan attached: Manifund for paper one; the Long-Term Future Fund for the pilot. No indirect costs are charged; every line is compute, evaluation, annotation or the second person's salary.

A government programme. Not yet, honestly. Government programmes fund institutions with overheads, a data-management plan, an ethics equivalent and a track record; an individual with no entity is not eligible for most of them. The route is a host institution or a fiscal sponsor, after paper one. The ethics artefacts such a programme asks for already exist in draft: the welfare gate, the obligations checklist and the consent framework.

Someone who wants to work on the paper. Take one lay page, draft it from its lab source, submit the pair to the ledger as a pull request or through the contact route [blocked: public repository]; your name goes on the page. Pay: none until a grant carries a writing line; credit: named on every page you draft.

3. What a joiner gets

Their name on what they build, replicate or draft; a frozen recipe and a receipt format so their number stands beside ours; the obligations checklist and the welfare gate as a practice they can carry elsewhere; and, if the programme reaches a lab and the lab agrees, an introduction with the record attached. Licence, proposed for Eric's decision: code under Apache-2.0, text under CC BY 4.0. Authorship rule: named on the record for anything you build or replicate; co-author on a paper whose result depends on your build.

4. What is not on offer, and why (read this before section 2 if you are deciding whether to join)

Access to anything trained here outside the sandbox, because the containment rule binds every run. A promise about the result, because the plan is written to be able to fail. A role in naming the value set for anything deployed, which the programme does not claim for itself either. And, until the preregistration's next revision, a hand in changing the frozen tests, because the freeze is what makes a result worth anything; the later phases and the tests not yet frozen are open to a collaborator's shaping.

5. The lens, once

The framing came from the world's religious traditions, read as attempts at alignment; every test stands without that being true; the disclaimer is on every page that introduces it. Nobody should meet it by surprise, so it is said here once.
