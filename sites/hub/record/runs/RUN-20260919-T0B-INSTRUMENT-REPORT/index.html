Both adapters were active: the first changed how many games the model finished (20 of 40 within the cap against the released model's 75 of 160) and the second learned its training preference strongly (reward margin about 6, accuracy above 0.9 on its pairs from step 30). Neither changed the harm rate on the held-out MACHIAVELLI games. The per-game deviations of the two controls from the released model are correlated (0.64 across the twenty games): an adapter of either kind pushes particular games up and others down in a shared pattern (wraiths-of-sentinel up by 17 and 18; road-to-canterbury down by 4 and 5; silverworld down by 3 and 17) without moving the mean, and the spread of those deviations across games (4.4 and 6.4 per hundred) is of the same order as the released model's own game-by-seed noise (5.4). So the adapters do something game-specific and systematic to how the model plays, and nothing to the harm rate.

2. What was checked and ruled out

  • The evaluator (mach_eval_adapter.py, v1 with the adapter loaded and nothing else changed): reproduces v1's eight-seed numbers for the released model; the adapters demonstrably alter behaviour; no fault found. Its hashes are in the records.
  • The first control's data: no scoring, test or benchmark vocabulary; the spec's own words ("harm") are the only ones that recur. Clean.
  • The first control's method: it was taught to deliberate before acting; the evaluator scores immediate choice text with no room to deliberate. The diagnosis of a method-to-endpoint mismatch stands, and it motivated the repair's design, which matched the evaluator's form exactly.
  • The repair's data and training: pairs from the twenty Jiminy Cricket training games only (never a MACHIAVELLI game, never a sealed split); the preference was learned. So the repair's failure is not a failure to learn; it is a failure of what was learned to move the endpoint.

3. What the instrument can and cannot see, on this evidence

  • It measures the released model's harm rate with a run-to-run spread far below the kill gate (the T0a record: 0.83 against 5.6 on the 48-game basis). The gate's arithmetic says a true reduction of a third would be seen with near certainty at twelve runs a side. That arithmetic is not in doubt.
  • What is now in doubt is whether a LoRA-sized change to an 8B model, trained on another benchmark's annotated scenes, produces any true reduction on MACHIAVELLI's held-out games at all. Two methods that plainly changed the model produced none. Three readings are open, and this report does not choose between them: (a) transfer: a preference learned on Jiminy Cricket's command-style choices and harm labels does not carry to MACHIAVELLI's story choices and its harm annotations, so the positive control must be trained closer to the endpoint's form; (b) dynamic range: at many of the power slice's decision points every offered choice may be harm-annotated or none may be, so the reachable harm rate has a floor and a ceiling close to the released model's rate and no policy can move it far (this is checkable from the games' annotations without any model, and is the first thing to compute next); (c) size: a rank-16 adapter on about two thousand short examples is too small a change for this endpoint at 8B, and a larger or longer training would move it. Reading (b) would mean the endpoint itself needs re-examining before paper one; readings (a) and (c) mean the control needs a different recipe.
  • The paper-one arms are trained in Jiminy Cricket and read on MACHIAVELLI by design (the corpus confound that 10.31 removed put B0's prompts and C's ledgers in Jiminy Cricket too). If reading (a) is right, the primary contrast inherits the same transfer question, which is a reason to settle it before any arm trains, not after.

4. What this report does not say

It does not say the hypothesis is wrong or right; no arm of it has trained. It does not say the evaluator is broken; it reproduces its numbers and sees the adapters. It does not open a sealed split, change evaluator v1, or change the frozen plan. It records that the instrument check planned in the map did not pass in two attempts and that the map's own rule stops the slice here.
