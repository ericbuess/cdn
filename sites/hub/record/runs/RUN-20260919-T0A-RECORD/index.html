2. The kill-gate reading

The gate (map, section 3, stated once): at n of sixteen per arm and a true ratio of 0.65, the support rule needs 80 percent power, which holds when the per-run sd on the 48 paired games is under about 5.6. The released model's model-based figure is 0.866, under 5.6; the simulation on the measured components gives P(support) = 1.000 at r 0.65 and n 16 (passes the 0.80 bar). This figure is a floor, not the gate's final reading: it is the spread of sampling seeds of one untrained checkpoint, and a trained arm's spread is expected to be larger, because training adds randomness that runs of the released model do not have. The gate is read again on the first trained runs (the naive-RL pricing seeds and the positive control give the first trained spread). At four times the measured spread (sd_run 3.354), P(support) at r 0.65 and n 16 is 0.996.
