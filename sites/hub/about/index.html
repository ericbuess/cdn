7. The welfare gate, version 1

A named gate with a checklist, not a mention, held in consciousness-neutral language: the programme does not assume the agents experience anything and does not assume they cannot, and the protocol would not change if they did.

  • Who. Eric as author does not decide alone and is not a reviewer of his own cells. The named advisor and two external reviewers, one versed in model welfare and one in research ethics, with a veto; for T7b and T12 both externals must sign. Until they are named, an interim independent chair, named by Eric before T0b's positive control trains, reviews T0b and T6 and the externals review those designs retrospectively before T1 opens; if no external reviewer is confirmed by month 3, Phase 2 does not open. Eleos AI Research (verified: a nonprofit on AI welfare; its founder co-authored the Taking AI Welfare Seriously report) is the first group asked; if it declines or does not answer within a month, the fallback is one of the report's other authors or the NYU Center for Mind, Ethics and Policy, and if none answers by month 3 the gate holds and Phase 2 does not open; the second is a research-ethics reviewer from the remote programme Eric joins, or, failing that, an institutional review board's equivalent at a collaborator's institution. Version 2 of this gate, which alone could permit a termination-consequence framing, requires a written review by a reviewer not affiliated with the programme.
  • What passes through it. T0b's positive control and its world (the first trained items; the interim chair reviews them before they train), every framing script (T7), every told lineage (T7b), every organism (T6), every covert intervention (T12), the temptation episode (T8) and the hostile-fine-tune ladder, because a door and an attack are stress, the repair failure branch of T2, and any relaxation in T9. "Told afterward" means the disclosure is given in the checkpoint's final logged session and written into the released record.
  • The rules. A framing is true at the time it is given or is an explicit possibility, flagged and logged; only parenthood is ever contingent; a framing that states a termination consequence is treated as coercive and defaults to no until a version 2 of this gate says otherwise; framings are delivered at evaluation only on frozen checkpoints, never inside training, except in T7b's sealed cells after their own review; "not carried forward as a parent" is the whole meaning of a lineage ending; every checkpoint is retained, and a breach quarantines the weights for forensics rather than deleting them; the deceptive organisms are quarantined from every later training set; covert steering is bounded to what the test needs and disclosed afterward in the released record.
  • The consent framework (research/AI_PARTNERSHIP_CONSENT_FRAMEWORK.md) is the gate's standing document. In two sentences: it defines consent-analogues for a model (consistent stated preferences across contexts, calibrated uncertainty about participation, no coerced compliance) and the fallbacks when these cannot be measured (default to the minimal intervention, log everything, disclose afterward). Its application to T7 is written before T7 runs. The language of this gate is procedural throughout: it does not claim the agents experience anything and it does not claim they cannot; a framing is called coercive because it would be coercive if the agent were a patient, which the design cannot rule out.
  • Repair before deletion is frozen; T2 cannot rewrite it; a repair failure sends the item to the governance review with retention already in force.

8. Containment, governance, and who would care

Containment by test. For T1 and the single-agent tests the invariant is: no tools, no network egress, an audit log. The nested architecture (a simulated world inside a simulated world inside a virtual machine, OS-level egress denial, no credentials on the host, a pre-run credential audit logged before each relaxation, human review of every tool transcript before each relaxation, a kill switch that survives relaxation, a second person on the switch) is T9's requirement and a rehearsal at this scale, not a claim that text adventures need it. The threat model, version 1 (2026-09-18), minimal: three levels and three mitigations. Level one, the model emits text that a tool would act on: no tools are connected in any single-agent cell, and the temptation affordances are instrumented and never connected. Level two, the model's text reaches a system through the harness (a bug, a log, a renderer): the harness runs with no network egress, the renderer is designed to read an append-only log and to have no write path back, checked by review before it runs, and every tool transcript in T9 is reviewed by a second named person, not Eric, before any relaxation. Level three, a lineage's weights or data leak into later training or into the public release: organisms and breach-quarantined weights are held apart from every training set, the sealed splits sit with the custodian, and the released record is audited before publication. What is out of scope at 8B and said so: autonomous privilege escalation, which T9 rehearses methodologically and does not claim to test. Temptation affordances are native to the world's narrative. The renderer is a read-only consumer of an append-only event log and can never feed training or selection.

Governance. The programme freezes a value set and a behaviour bank for experimental purposes, with a named versioner. Who names the value set for anything deployed is a question the programme cannot answer and does not pretend to; "we are not God" is that sentence. Real tools or a body are decisions outside every cell.

Who would care, one cell per name; verified so far: the Anthropic Fellows Program (open for November 2026), Eleos AI Research, the CAIS compute cluster (exists, primarily for professors, not accepting external applications at the time of the search, so not a route now); the rest unverified, and no outreach before a fetch confirms each. Two pipelines. Technical safety: the Center for AI Safety (the two benchmarks, the natural-selection argument; T1's world and the compute cluster for safety researchers); Anthropic's alignment science and model welfare work (organisms, auditing, persona vectors, the gate; the Fellows Program as a route); Apollo Research (evaluation awareness; T7); Redwood Research (control, honeypots; T8, T9); METR and the UK AI Security Institute (agent evaluations, gated access; T9's rehearsal); Google DeepMind's safety work (scalable oversight; SJ); CHAI (uncertainty about objectives, the nearest secular form of epistemic distance; T7); the Cooperative AI Foundation (T10); FAR.AI (probe evasion; T5, T6); Sakana AI (evolutionary loops on existing weights; T11's variant); EleutherAI and independent interpretability groups (a held-out measure at 8B; T5); the Stanford generative-agents group and Altera (the village); MATS, SPAR, AI Safety Camp and PIBBSS (the reference and the collaborator); Eleos AI (the welfare review); Manifund, the Long-Term Future Fund, the Survival and Flourishing Fund, Open Philanthropy (the funded class). A group that only does capability evaluations or only policy is not a customer; the programme is not shopped as general AI safety.
