Proposed blue-team program

From AI-found bugs to installed protection

The blue team is the hardening program: installed protection for the systems people already run.

A useful fix still needs review, a distribution route, an owner willing to install it and evidence that it reduced exposure.

The proposal

A frontier lab could sponsor a 90-day pilot with consenting maintainers and operators in one supported web-service deployment class. Connect the whole path: inventory, authorized changes, isolated testing, approval, canary deployment, verification and recovery.

Defensive agents use scoped credentials and audit logs outside their write authority. Patch author, verifier and deployment authority stay separate.

Evidence that would matter

Installed protection

Measure installed protection, time to deployment, operator effort, regressions, recovery and remaining attack paths.

Declared comparison

Compare against the operator’s normal process under declared resource budgets.

Honest limits

Keep offered, eligible and successfully protected systems separate. Preserve rejected cases and missing evidence. A finite test with no successful attacks is not proof of safety.
