AI safety · research · practical tools

Eric Buess

I work on AI safety: how to evaluate increasingly capable systems, understand where safeguards fall short, and turn promising ideas into tests others can inspect and build on.

My current work includes the Alignment Hypothesis and proposals for independent evaluation. I’m also building Go Screenless and tools for coordinating AI agents, both to support this research and to make useful work easier to do by voice. I care about careful work, honest uncertainty, and leaving time for family and the things that matter.

Current work ranges from research proposals to working prototypes. Each project shows what has been built, what has been tested, and what remains to be done.

How I work

Capable systems, independent challenge, human judgment.

Draft with multiple models.

I use models from several providers to develop and challenge ideas, keeping the questions and source material available for review.

Inspect and test the output.

I compare claims with their sources and turn disagreements into questions that can be tested.

Keep the decision human.

I keep responsibility for consequential decisions and a record of what changed and why.
