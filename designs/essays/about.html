About

I’m Eric Buess. I work on AI safety, including the Alignment Hypothesis, and build tools for research and working with AI by voice. The research is early, and Go Screenless is coming soon. I’ve used a pendant and local phone transcription in my earlier Titus system, and I’m working toward making that experience easier for others to set up. I’ve mentored students through Inspire, given talks, helped small businesses and mayoral candidates adopt AI, shared open-source work and joined podcasts. I care about work people can inspect and build on, with time left for family.
