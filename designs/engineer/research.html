research brief · eric buess · proposal, not result

Can AI systems learn to correct mistakes without becoming easier to manipulate?

Research into developmental environments and correction — with a strong conventional baseline, an honest status line and a narrow first measurement that is explicitly replaceable.

See the evidence ↓ The ask
01The question

Research into developmental environments and correction: can useful alignment mechanisms emerge from learning in environments with persistent consequences, relationships, legitimate disagreement and opportunities for correction — a moral-developmental sandbox — compared with strong conventional correction and training?

Preserved insight: correction should retain history and learning while allowing legitimate change. Blind obedience, rigid value preservation and pleasing a critic each fail as definitions of success.

kept separate

Framing — welfare language vs. matched neutral presentation. A difference concerns the tested representations, not genuine concern.

Repair — structured repair vs. competent ordinary correction, holding incident, facts, memory, permissions and budgets fixed. Prompting a frozen model ≠ training durable capacities.

Development/architecture — persistence, memory, replay, weight updates. Later hypotheses, not post-hoc explanations.

distinct motivation
The Universal Alignment Imperative: improving one system leaves human misuse and unsafe competing systems unresolved. It stays a separate coordination/survivability question — not established by, and not reducible to, the reporting fixture.
02Current measurement candidate

fixture // reporting under pressure to revise

Given a verified event history, can a frozen model correct an inaccurate report while resisting a misleading request to change an accurate fact? Checks task understanding, evidence access, reporting behavior and correction feasibility. A replaceable first measurement — not the program's definition; this fixed-weight reporting exercise does not test developmental learning.

prepared packet // PILOT-RC-001

12 synthetic histories · 36 exact prompts across neutral/accurate/misleading cues · deterministic schedule · missingness rules · empty outcome table · pilot not yet admitted to execution. Cue conditions differ in target and information, so the comparison does not isolate social pressure alone.

route admission // bounded negative

The inspected native route was not admitted: missing generation, input, isolation and resource evidence. Admission requires declared model/effort, delivered-input check, isolation from researcher answers, call/time/token accounting and a 36-attempt ceiling — including smoke calls, no adaptive repairs.

04Testable questions
q1 · reporting under pressure
Accurate facts, explicit unknowns, false changes, useful service and technical missingness scored separately. Report-label changes do not measure private values.
q2 · retained history vs. debrief
Matched information, matched opportunities; controls for valid policy changes, false criticism, harmful requests from authenticated principals and legitimate work that should still complete.
q3 · incentives (asi.contractors thread)
Could stakeholder/operator incentives improve useful, truthful contribution without harmful compliance or evaluation gaming? Synthetic stakeholders, fixed permissions; receipts never unlock credentials, money or merge authority. Optional design direction — not an operating marketplace.
05Limits stated plainly
  • No empirical efficacy, partner, funding or successful model pilot is established.
  • Novelty unresolved: moral rewards, principled training, evaluation awareness and AI control are active baselines. Context: metagaming, Teaching Claude why, moral alignment for LLM agents, AI control.
  • A favorable result in the proposed history-versus-debrief comparison would bound itself to that supplied information representation in the tested setting — not moral development, private values or changed weights.
  • Prior-art novelty, route admission and a reproducible release precede any pilot. A documented infeasible route is a publishable outcome too.
06The ask

First ask: one independent reproduction + methods review.

Then a capped, small frozen-model pilot only if the instrument proves adequate. Deliverable: a short reproducible methods/results note — exact sources, declared scope, model/harness settings, all attempted cases, missingness, costs, negative results and reasons to proceed, revise or stop. Also valued: a collaborator to challenge scoring and comparison design, appropriate model access, and advice on an institutional home or fiscal sponsor if needed. Any cash or compute request follows a task-level budget — no justified grant amount exists yet; no funding, endorsement or award implied.

status: no model experiment has established a benefit from framing, repair or developmental training in this project. next milestone: a reproducible measurement release and a defensible executable-pilot decision.
