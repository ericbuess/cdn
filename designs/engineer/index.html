I teach, build and study AI — including how it corrects its own mistakes.

One record from two sides: making powerful tools understandable and useful, and asking how more capable systems stay truthful and correctable. The Alignment Hypothesis is the research. Go Screenless is the voice interface for directing and hearing the work.

01Current focus

the alignment hypothesis

Research into developmental environments and correction: whether persistent consequences, relationships and opportunities for legitimate correction help AI systems stay useful, truthful and correctable. Proposal + initial measurement tools; no empirical result yet.
